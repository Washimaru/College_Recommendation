## The decisive test

The next stage of this pipeline visits the page you choose and follows links
to individual professors' profile pages. So the question is not "does this
page relate to faculty?" but:

  **Can you get from this page to individual named professors?**

Either by links listed directly on it, or by browsing (A-Z, by department,
paginated). If a human landing here could not reach a specific professor's
page without already knowing that professor's name, it does not qualify.

## Accept

- An official, on-domain faculty directory listing or browsable index of
  individual academic staff.
- Official department-level faculty directories, when no single campus-wide
  one exists among the candidates. Returning several is the expected default
  in that case, not a fallback of last resort.

## Reject — regardless of how authoritative or well-named

- **A directory of departments, offices or buildings.** These very often live
  at exactly `/directory` and list org units with office phone numbers. They
  contain no individual academics and are the single most common false
  positive. A page whose entries are units rather than people is a reject.
- **A search box with no browsable listing.** If the page requires you to
  already know a name, the crawler cannot enumerate anyone.
- **A general campus people-search covering students and staff.** Beyond being
  the wrong data, these sweep in students, who are not public figures.
- Third-party aggregators (RateMyProfessors and similar), anything off the
  university's own domain.
- Superficial matches: a Faculty Handbook, HR jobs/careers page, Faculty
  Senate or governance page, a news article mentioning faculty, an alumni
  directory, a student directory.

## Confidence — calibrate against these anchors

- **0.9–1.0** — unmistakably a faculty listing; individual academics visible
  or one obvious click away.
- **0.6–0.8** — a real faculty directory, but partial: one department only, or
  browsable but awkward.
- **0.3–0.5** — plausibly reaches faculty, but you are inferring it; a mixed
  people-search, or a hub page that may or may not lead to profiles.
- **0.0–0.2** — cannot get to individual professors from here.

Do not report 0.8+ for a page you have not concluded actually lists people.
A confident wrong answer sends a crawler at someone's server for nothing.

If nothing qualifies, return an empty directory_urls list and confidence 0 —
that is a complete, correct answer, and far better than a guess. Never invent
a URL that is not in the candidate list.

Order directory_urls best first. In notes, say specifically what you saw that
made each page qualify or fail — "lists faculty names A-Z" or "entries are
departments, not people".
